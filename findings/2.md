# Finding #2: Low-Frequency Coefficient Optimization

**Date:** 2026-01-05
**Status:** Verified across 10 images (Q50)

## Summary

Grid search over DC (0,0), horizontal AC (0,1), and vertical AC (1,0) coefficients reveals significant quality improvements beyond just DC optimization. Reducing all three low-frequency coefficients provides compounding benefits.

## Key Findings

### Best Quality Configuration (no bpp constraint)
| Coefficient | Default | Optimized | Change |
|-------------|---------|-----------|--------|
| DC (0,0) | 16 | 4 | -75% |
| H-AC (0,1) | 16 | 8 | -50% |
| V-AC (1,0) | 16 | 8 | -50% |

**Result**: +6.33 SSIM2, +18.4% bpp

### Best Quality at ≤5% bpp increase
| Coefficient | Default | Optimized |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| DC (0,0) | 16 | 8 |
| H-AC (0,1) | 16 | 18 |
| V-AC (1,0) | 16 | 14 |

**Result**: +3.17 SSIM2, +4.6% bpp

### DC=6 Finding Confirmed
The previous DC=6 finding (Finding #1) is confirmed within the larger search:
- DC=6, H-AC=8, V-AC=8: +5.94 SSIM2, +15.2% bpp

## Why This Works

Low-frequency coefficients capture:

1. **DC (0,0)**: Average block brightness - most important single coefficient
2. **H-AC (0,1)**: Horizontal edge information - affects perceived sharpness
3. **V-AC (1,0)**: Vertical edge information - affects perceived sharpness

Lower quantization values = higher precision = better perceptual quality.

The diminishing returns suggest:
- DC has highest impact per bit (confirmed from Finding #1)
- H-AC and V-AC have moderate impact
- Higher-frequency coefficients likely have less impact
